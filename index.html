A little
letter for you. ♡

Some things are easier to say when you write them down. So... this one's for you.
For the girl who somehow became my favorite person.

Yara,

Sometimes I think about how weird it is that something this important to me started so simply.

A profile picture. A group chat. A few conversations. One DM. A first date. Three days where we tried to stay away from each other and completely failed.

None of those moments looked like they were going to change my life when they happened. But together, they gave me you. And I don't think I'll ever stop being grateful for that.

You became someone I look forward to talking to, someone I miss when you're not around, someone who can make me laugh over the dumbest things, and someone whose little habits and random jokes somehow became some of my favorite parts of my day.

I love the serious moments with you. But I also love the stupid ones. The jokes that make absolutely no sense. The random conversations. The “I don't want” moments. The chickens. The meowing. The pictures. The things that probably wouldn't mean anything to anyone else. Because they're ours.

And I want you to know something. I'm not grateful only for the day we became official. I'm grateful for every little moment that brought me there. Because if you took away even one of those moments... we might not have become us.

So I don't just love the person I get to call my girlfriend. I love the story that brought me to you. And I love the fact that our story is still being written.

I don't know exactly what every next page will look like. But I know I want to be there for it. The cute pages. The chaotic pages. The pages where we're laughing over something stupid. The pages where we miss each other. The pages we haven't even imagined yet.

And if I get to keep writing them with you... then honestly, I don't need to know how the story ends yet.

I love you, Yara. ♡
— Wael
